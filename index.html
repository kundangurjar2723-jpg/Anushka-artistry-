Hi, I’m Anushka — an artist who loves turning everyday inspiration into pencil art. From expressive portraits to nature-inspired studies, I enjoy the patience, practice and little discoveries that happen with every drawing.

This portfolio is a growing collection of my work, my experiments and the details that catch my eye. Thank you for being here and taking a closer look.
